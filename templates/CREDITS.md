## Rules

- **Allowed:** CC0, public domain, CC-BY with attribution, assets the user owns
  or supplied, and AI-generated meshes the user commissioned (Meshy, Tripo, etc).
- **Not allowed without asking:** anything NC, ND, "editorial use only", ripped
  from a game, or with no stated licence. Ask the user; do not guess.
- CC-BY needs visible attribution in the page footer or an `About` line, not just
  this file.
- Record the URL you actually downloaded from, and the date, because listings
  move.
- Note the edits you made: retopology, reskin, rescaled, root motion stripped.
